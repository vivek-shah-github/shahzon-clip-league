SHAHZON CLIP LEAGUE · Season 01

Official rules

Pilot duration: 30 days. Rules for a running season are not changed retroactively; future seasons may be revised.

Eligibility & entry

  • Participation is free.
  • Submit your own account and clip URL and agree to the rules.
  • No subscription, like, comment, membership or purchase is required to enter or win.
  • We collect only what's needed. If you are under 18, follow platform age rules and involve a parent or guardian.
  • No fake views, bots, purchased engagement, spam, impersonation or copied submissions.

Eligible clip types

  • BGMI clutch plays and close fights.
  • Funny facecam reactions, unexpected fails and squad moments.
  • Challenges, mini-stories, tips or unusual gameplay situations.
  • Clips must include meaningful editing, context, captions, pacing or storytelling. A raw re-upload of a segment does not qualify.

Publishing requirements

  • Publish on your own YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels or another approved platform.
  • Credit SHAHZON clearly and link the official channel where the platform allows.
  • Use only footage you're authorized to use and follow the written permission terms.
  • Don't imply you are the official channel or speak for SHAHZON.
  • No misleading titles, harassment, slurs, personal information, or third-party copyrighted material without rights.

Disqualification

A clip may be disqualified if it is deleted, private, misleading, copied, artificially inflated or submitted after the deadline.

Submission limit: up to 3 eligible entries per clipper per week.

Tie-break & disputes

  • Tie-break 1: higher editing + originality score.
  • Tie-break 2: higher consistency & compliance score.
  • You get 48 hours after provisional results to flag factual scoring errors.
  • If a dispute remains, the organiser makes a documented final decision based on these rules.

Safety & rights

Platform compliance

  • Use of SHAHZON stream footage is limited to the written permission terms, including whether clips may be monetized.
  • Permission to use footage doesn't guarantee monetization. Platforms judge reused or repetitive content separately.
  • Bulk reposting of identical clips is not allowed.
  • Disclose paid or incentivized promotion where the platform or law requires it.
  • YouTube and Instagram are not sponsors of this league and are not involved in running it.
  • This league is a community pilot run by SHAHZON and may be revised for future seasons.
Clipper guide

Anatomy of a winning clip

Start at the most interesting moment. Keep only the context needed. End with a natural pointer to the channel.

0–2sHook

Open on the moment, or set up a clear question. No long intros.

MainThe moment

Only the context needed to understand the play or reaction.

ThroughoutEditing

Readable captions, clean pacing, balanced audio, vertical framing.

Last 2sEnding + credit

Point to SHAHZON's daily streams. Credit and link.

Checklist before you post

  • Start with the most interesting moment; avoid long intros.
  • Add readable captions and edit for clear pacing.
  • Make the clip understandable without misleading context.
  • Credit SHAHZON and link the official channel where possible.
  • Submit the clip URL and the source stream timestamp.
  • Use only permitted footage and original or authorized assets.
  • Follow the current season rules and deadlines.
  • Never buy views or copy another creator's edit.

Publishing tips

  • Test several formats instead of posting many near-identical clips.
  • Use accurate titles and relevant captions. Skip misleading clickbait.
  • Keep the official channel link in your profile or caption.
  • SHAHZON won't repost every clip or endorse every clipper.
